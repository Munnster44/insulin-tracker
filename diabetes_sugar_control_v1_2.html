Read Me — Version 1.2

1. Enter a reading: Open Add Reading, confirm date and time, choose Morning / Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Bedtime / Other, enter glucose, any actual insulin dosages, and notes. Use Mounjaro dose only when recording an actual weekly injection.

2. Decision field: +2, +1, Hold, -1, or -2 units is a manual log for a proposed or clinician-approved change. The software does not predict or prescribe insulin doses.

3. Review progress: Dashboard shows average, percent of recorded readings in your chosen range, and highs/lows. Change time period and reading type to examine patterns.

4. Daily graph: On Dashboard, select any date or use previous/next/today/latest. The 24-hour graph shows sugar readings, meal/injection markers, the target band, and the 4.0 safety line. The daily table lists exact times and doses. The graph displays only recorded observations, not continuous glucose monitoring.

5. Edit existing readings: In the Dashboard daily details or recent readings, or the History table, select Edit. Correct any fields including date, time, glucose, medication doses, and notes, then choose Save changes. Clear / New record cancels editing without saving. Changes update the graph and summaries automatically.

6. Maintain medication names: Settings lets you customize labels for the two insulins. Mounjaro is tracked in mg, whereas the other insulin doses are in units.

7. Injection tracking: Select the Mounjaro strength when you actually take it. The Mounjaro tab summarizes the last logged injection and shows an illustrative 10% calculation only.

8. Export and restore: History exports visible readings to CSV. Settings downloads a full JSON backup, exports all readings to CSV, or restores a JSON backup.

9. Safety: A glucose reading below 4.0 mmol/L is low. If alert and able to swallow, follow your prescribed hypoglycemia plan; Diabetes Canada suggests 15 g of fast-acting carbohydrate and repeat testing after 10–15 minutes. If severely confused, unconscious, or unable to swallow safely, get emergency help. Never rely on this app for real-time alarms or dose changes.

10. Understanding range: The app calculates percentage of recorded readings in target, not continuous time in range. A CGM is needed for a more representative time-in-range estimate. A 90–95% target within 5–7 may be difficult and unsafe for some people, especially when using insulin. Review it with your clinical team.

Privacy: This is a single-file offline app. No account, cloud server, or automatic sync. Browser storage is specific to the browser and device.
